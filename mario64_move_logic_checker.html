How the checker interprets logic

It evaluates the JSON's AND/OR expressions, parenthesized groups, move/item tokens, star-count thresholds, and recursive @Macro references. Difficulty tokens such as |!hard| are true when the corresponding mode is enabled. A star is considered accessible only if its level entry requirement and its own star requirement both pass. A cannon uses the level entry requirement plus its cannon requirement. Items such as keys and caps are selected in the same inventory list as moves.

This is a requirement checker, not a full Archipelago fill simulation. It does not infer that you can collect more stars, keys, or moves from locations shown as accessible; you must update the player state yourself.
